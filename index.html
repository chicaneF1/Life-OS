Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

How to operate Life OS as your personal execution cockpit with zero friction.

🌅 Phase 1: Morning Priming (06:00 - 08:00)

Never open social media first. Open Life OS Command Center.

  • Lock The ONE Thing: Write down the single action that guarantees today's win.
  • Inspect Daily Blocks: Review Morning, Afternoon, and Evening actions.
  • Hydrate & Sunlight: Knock out your first physical recovery routine immediately.
☀️ Phase 2: Execution & Deep Work (09:00 - 18:00)

Execute actions by time block. Avoid multitasking.

  • Check off as you do: Mark actions done. The visual timestamp confirms it's finished.
  • Log Protein & Water: Tap +25g protein scoops or +500ml water in the Recovery tab.
  • Access Vault: Tap 🔗 Resources on any Goal/Action to view linked YouTube videos or notes.
🌙 Phase 3: Evening Calibration (21:00 - 22:00)

Close the psychological loop before sleeping (under 2 minutes).

  • Daily Check-in: Rate Mood, Energy, and Focus (1 to 5). Note today's win & friction.
  • Log Goal Notes: Add a 1-line dated note on active goals if you made progress today.
  • Prepare Tomorrow: Leave workout clothes or reading material ready by your bedside.

🔄 The Sunday Calibration & Accountability Protocol

1. System Triage Rule

If a system drops below 50% completion, do not beat yourself up. Modify the system! Reduce frequency (e.g. 5x/wk to 3x/wk) or lower friction. We fall to the level of our systems.

2. WhatsApp Accountability Loop

Every goal card has an Accountability Partner button. On Sunday, click it to automatically draft a WhatsApp message with your current target, percentage, and pledge for the week.
